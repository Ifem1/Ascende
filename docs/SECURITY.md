# Security and release status

**Release verdict: NOT READY for production-safe escrow.** The remaining transfer and evidence-provenance limitations below are release blockers. Stable Studionet is used for test evidence only.

## Verified contract controls

- Terms are frozen in a zero-value draft. The named recipient must accept before the original funder can activate with the exact budget. Unaccepted and accepted-but-unfunded drafts expire permissionlessly and hold no principal. After activation, milestones progress in order; only final qualification vests its tranche.
- Evidence URLs must match the frozen HTTPS path authority. The requested response must be status 200 and match the committed byte length and SHA-256 during leader and validator review. A mismatch is `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`, never a business denial.
- Every new milestone requires at least two required authorities on distinct hostnames; evidence and challenge manifests commit each required URL, exact byte length and SHA-256. Each criterion/source finding also contains a zero-based UTF-8 byte span and quotation that the contract matches against those fetched bytes. This supplies citation traceability, not proof of publisher identity or truth. Each validator independently fetches every frozen byte set and reports a source-level finding for every required authority against every criterion. A criterion is supported or contradicted only when at least two required authorities agree and none of the cited authorities conflicts; mixed findings are INSUFFICIENT. Leader and validator must agree on the exact evidence root, criterion outcomes, and source-level findings. This makes consensus decision-critical: a leader-only result or disagreement over provenance cannot vest a tranche. Distinct hostnames do not establish distinct owners or organizational independence.
- Challenge records preserve the original and challenged manifests, roots, prior result, criterion packet, bond owner, and timestamps. Decision and program histories retain retry attempts and terminal outcomes. Frozen retry spacing and grace windows bound submitted and challenged reviews; unresolved challenge closure returns the locked bond to its challenger.
- Python `datetime.now(timezone.utc)` is bound by GenVM to the transaction context timestamp and is identical across validators; it is not a validator host clock. Deadline comparisons use that transaction timestamp. See [GenLayer transaction context](https://docs.genlayer.com/developers/intelligent-contracts/features/transaction-context).
- Withdrawals, refunds, and bond claims reserve a unique immutable operation before the one permitted EOA child transfer. Submitted and ambiguous operations remain pending and cannot be resent from contract state.
- In the current source, `create_program` and `challenge_milestone` perform validation before writes and catch only an explicit allowlist of expected `gl.vm.UserError` rejection codes. A rejection returns zero, records the original sender, exact attached value and reason, and creates no program/challenge entitlement. Unknown `UserError`s are re-raised; other exception classes are not caught. Direct Mode tests cover this path and the credit conservation invariant.
- Rejected-payable credits can be reserved only by their original sender. Each reservation creates a unique operation with the sender as immutable destination; submission moves the amount to pending before emitting one child transfer. There is no delivery confirmation or retry. The current deployment's live rejection/refund demonstration is recorded in [LIVE_VERIFICATION.md](LIVE_VERIFICATION.md); the older address below does not contain these methods.

## Known limitations and release blockers

- The pinned Web Access response surface used here exposes status and body, but not an enforceable final response URL or a no-redirect request option. Authority checks therefore establish the requested URL, not the final redirect destination. Final source authority has **not** been verified. Do not treat current URL-prefix checks as redirect protection.
- Prompt-injection resistance is prompt guidance plus independent source-level classification agreement, not a security boundary. Multiple hostnames may share ownership or infrastructure; sources may be compromised, false, or mutually copied; correlated model errors remain possible. Consensus is a rule for accepting the agreed packet, not proof of truth.
- GenLayer EOA child transfers are asynchronous and have no authenticated child-result callback in this contract. `mark_transfer_unresolved` is only an uncertainty marker; it does not prove failure, return funds, or permit retry. A parent success, child lookup, explorer status, or off-chain balance observation cannot safely authorize a second send on-chain.
- Older deployed addresses do not contain the new rejection-credit methods. Known unrecoverable failed-call surplus remains 0.0002 GEN on `0x1cC1130daeA4a26f0be748063a903d254b4CCe96`, 0.06 GEN on `0xB8c2965026223a39d78f3834FBe0646cB3747C21`, and 5 wei on `0xC2F0F3E18B69F398385652A55F423a1e339b4290`; none is recoverable through those deployed interfaces.
- The historical test deployment `0x03b3525C550E5281747E686b87d19aDbD5f4F10b` has 0.0201 GEN of finalized EOA children represented as pending in its contract ledger: 0.01 GEN earned payout, 0.0001 GEN bond, and 0.01 GEN refund. Each corresponding child receipt reports finalized and `value_credited=true`, but no contract callback can safely reconcile the ledger. The prior diagnostic deployment `0xb47Cc771D53846246016D78D39e59AAC0884D827` has 0.0101 GEN in the same unresolved accounting state. These are delivered test transfers with unresolved on-chain status, not evidence of recoverable escrow.
- The current branch is deployed at `0x5715eACA23d495b71E800Dd9eD6e13875855bd9c`; its source-byte comparison is documented in [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md). A fresh create attempt finalized without creating a program, and no successful lifecycle has yet been demonstrated at this address. The prior source-matched deployment `0x2c7a6260f2958C0Bd6634182E9978d209F0a06aD` belongs to commit `fccac05730c95d68f5759c89f9e689c0d76165b5`; its receipts remain historical and do not verify the current ABI.
- Superseded stable deployment: `0x884676bCDE191A9D9c79B3111812425E15824836`. A fresh invalid payable create credited exactly 2 wei to that sender's rejection ledger; a single authorized refund submission produced a child receipt with `value_credited=true`, while that older contract's accounting remained pending and unverifiable. The historical `0xC2F0F3E18B69F398385652A55F423a1e339b4290` deployment has 5 wei of observed failed payable value without matching state: two rejected program creations of 2 wei each and one expired challenge call of 1 wei. It has no rejection-credit recovery methods; those funds remain stranded. Six successful 2-wei children and two successful 1-wei bond children each show `value_credited=true`, while that contract records 12 wei in principal transfers and 2 wei in bond transfers as pending; all six programs have zero locked principal. These are live child observations, not reconciled balances. Exact historical receipts are in [LIVE_VERIFICATION.md](LIVE_VERIFICATION.md).
- Consequently, the obligation ledger is not a proof of physical solvency or lossless EOA delivery. A new contract deployment does not repair balances stranded at old addresses. No automatic resend, unauthenticated failure assertion, or admin sweep is safe to add.

The current branch does not add an EVM settlement vault. The mandatory six-part stable-runtime proof was not completed: official GenLayer documentation describes EVM contract calls and synchronous views, but the read-only Studionet checks did not deploy a Solidity contract, exercise a Ghost-originated write, test idempotent settlement, or verify an operation from GenVM. The public RPC returned `eth_accounts` method-not-found; `eth_chainId` returned `0xf22f`. No supported failure has been proven, so the architecture is unverified rather than declared impossible. Until the complete live proof and a reviewed vault implementation pass, outgoing EOA delivery remains once-only and unreconciled. Ascende is not production-safe escrow.
